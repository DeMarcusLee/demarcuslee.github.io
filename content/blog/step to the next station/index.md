After a rewarding period as a postdoctoral researcher at the Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA), I am moving on to the next stage of my academic journey.

I have joined the **School of Statistics and Mathematics at Zhongnan University of Economics and Law**. In this new position, I will continue my research in theoretical statistics and machine learning, while exploring new problems and opportunities for collaboration.

I expect to begin teaching in the **Fall 2027 semester**. My office is located in **Room 516, Shuangchuang Building**.

I look forward to this new chapter.
