abstract: >
  This paper develops a rigorous framework for establishing the sharp minimax
  optimality of both sparse group LASSO and sparse group SLOPE under double
  sparsity. We derive an original upper bound for the Gaussian complexity
  associated with a weighted combination of the element-wise and group-wise
  penalties. Based on this result, we establish sharp optimality results for
  sparse group LASSO and sparse group SLOPE under restricted eigenvalue-type
  conditions. Our analysis shows that the intersection of the sparse restricted
  eigenvalue cones associated with element-wise sparsity and group-wise sparsity
  corresponds to the solution of a weighted $\ell_1+\ell_{1,2}$ penalty. We further
  derive optimal sample complexity results under random designs, considering both
  weak moment assumptions and sub-Gaussian random designs. Numerical experiments
  confirm that the proposed methods perform favorably compared with methods based
  on only one type of sparsity assumption.
